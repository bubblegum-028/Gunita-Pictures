01 — ABOUT

Gunita Pictures was built from a passion for capturing life.

At 19, Jai started Gunita Pictures with nothing more than a passion for photography and a love for capturing moments that matter. What began with kiddie photography in Laguna became the foundation of a journey that would grow far beyond its beginnings.

Over the past four years, Jai has expanded into capturing different kinds of events, from celebrations and family gatherings to weddings and corporate stories. What once started as a small service offered only in Laguna has grown into a team serving clients across Laguna, Cavite, Batangas, and Metro Manila.

Today, Gunita Pictures continues to grow while staying true to the passion that started it all — capturing genuine moments, telling stories through photographs and films, and preserving memories that last.
